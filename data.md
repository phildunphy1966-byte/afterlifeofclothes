## Definitions
Pre-consumer: waste before product reaches consumer.
Post-consumer: textile discarded after use.
Reuse: product continues in use.
Recycling: material/fibre recovered for another production cycle.
Downcycling: textile becomes lower-value material/product.
Disposal: incineration or landfill in this simplified model.

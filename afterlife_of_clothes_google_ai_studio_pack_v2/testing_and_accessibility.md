# Testing & Accessibility
Test with 5–8 intended users. Ask what the site is about; what 58% means; difference between reuse/recycling/downcycling; what happened to their T-shirt; which interaction confused them; what statistic they remember.

Functional QA: every CTA works; restart clears choices; sources open; no fake survey values; mobile does not require hover; charts resize without clipping.

Aim for WCAG AA: semantic headings, contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, text alternatives, no colour-only encoding, reduced motion, ~44px touch targets, readable mobile type.

Data ethics: show source/year near statistics; label estimates; do not imply causation from survey correlations; do not present student survey as nationally representative; anonymise responses.

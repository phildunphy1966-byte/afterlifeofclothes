## Decision
# What would you do with it?
Donate / Sell or give away / Repurpose / Throw away.
**Your decision determines where the garment enters the next system — but not necessarily where it finally ends.**

# Clothes don't sort themselves.
Condition · Material · Colour · Quality · Resale potential · Recycling potential.

## Definitions
**Reuse:** garment remains clothing.
**Recycle:** material/fibre recovered for another production cycle.
**Downcycle:** textile becomes a lower-value material/product.
**Disposal:** incineration or landfill.

## Outcome
# Where does post-consumer textile waste end up?
**4.10 million tonnes/year**
**55% → reuse / recycling / downcycling**
**45% → incineration / landfill**

## Comparison
# Something changes after we buy it.
Pre-consumer: **2.973M tonnes**, about **2%** mapped to incineration/landfill.
Post-consumer: **4.10M tonnes**, about **45%** mapped to incineration/landfill.

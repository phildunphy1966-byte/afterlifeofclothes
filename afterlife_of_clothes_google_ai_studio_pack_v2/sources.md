# Sources

## Ministry of Textiles, Government of India (2026)
**Mapping of Textile Waste Value Chain in India**
https://www.texmin.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/407c2f186a2044a4497c9c9803d16a2c.pdf
Core source for national waste quantities, source split, collection pathways and mapped outcomes.

## United Nations Environment Programme
https://www.unep.org/ietc/news/statement/press-release-unsustainable-fashion-and-textiles-focus-international-day-zero-waste
Use for global context.

## Fashion for Good (2022)
**Wealth in Waste: India's Potential to Bring Textile Waste Back into the Supply Chain**
https://www.fashionforgood.com/report/wealth-in-waste/
Supporting India research. Do not combine its estimate with the 2026 Ministry estimate as a simple time series.

## Foundation for MSME Clusters (2026)
https://fmc.org.in/panipat-textile-recycling-sustainability-readiness-report/
Use for Panipat cluster context.

Every major visualisation should show a short source label and open a full Sources/Method drawer on click.

## Kaggle — Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nicapotato/womens-ecommerce-clothing-reviews
23,486 reviews; anonymised real commercial data; CC0. Supporting consumer-review context only.

## Kaggle — Clothing Fit Data
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pypiahmad/clothing-fit-data
Original research dataset information: https://mcauleylab.ucsd.edu/public_datasets/data/datasets.html
Supporting fit context only.

## Kaggle / Zenodo — Fast Fashion Eco-Data
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/fast-fashion-eco-data
https://zenodo.org/records/4261101
277 Spanish Zara catalogue items captured in 2020; illustrative composition/catalogue data only.
